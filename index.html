Strawberry Rhubarb Chia Pudding

This vegan dessert is fresh, fun, and easy to prepare.

  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Cook time: 15 minutes
  • Servings: 3

Ingredients

Pudding

  • 3 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 cup plant-based milk (almond, oat, or soy)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 can full-fat coconut milk, refrigerated

Strawberry Rhubarb Jam

  • 1 cup strawberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 cup rhubarb (fresh or frozen)
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh squeezed lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Combine chia seeds, milk, and vanilla in a jar. Stir and refrigerate overnight.
  2. Remove from fridge in the morning.
  3. Prepare jam: simmer strawberries, rhubarb, lemon juice, and sugar on low for 15 minutes.
  4. Skim thick coconut cream from can and whisk until smooth.
  5. If pudding is too thick, mix in a spoonful of coconut milk or water.
  6. Layer in jars: jam, chia pudding, whipped coconut cream. Repeat.
  7. Top with more jam and berries.
